prj - Opinionated todo-list application for the unix CLI.
Simon Danielsson <contact@simondanielsson.se>
https://github.com/simon-danielsson/prj

USAGE:
prj <command> [id]

* Most editing is done by calling an external text-editor. Set environment variable
  '$EDITOR' to your preferred editor (neovim, for example).
* Perpetual storage found at '$HOME/.prj.storage'.
